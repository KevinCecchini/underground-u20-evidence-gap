# Evidence-to-Opportunity Gap (EOG): research protocol

**Original research design, NOT a validated proprietary algorithm.**

## Decision layers
1. Identity and role (first-party club/team evidence preferred; conflicting labels retained).
2. Deployment (dated official lineups, substitutions, age group, documented senior appearances).
3. On-field production (only after coverage and minutes validation; rates without reliable denominators are prohibited).
4. External exposure (first-team, youth international involvement, transfer, contractual lock-in).
5. Recruitability (contract, attainable club tier, financial terms, tactical fit) — not estimated in this release.
6. Negative evidence, missingness, and confidence: high exposure can reduce *market opportunity* even when sporting promise is strong.

## Gates
* OBSERVE: at least a first-party identity/role source and dated playing evidence; request full video; *not buy recommendation*.
* INVESTIGATE: identity/role confirmed but minutes, appearance, or season coverage missing.
* BENCHMARK: relatively exposed or restricted; compare development but do not label undervalued.
* EXCLUDE ROLE: outside requested winger position or unable to verify it.

## Anti-hype policy
No made-up performance score; no inferred successful dribbles from goals; no price speculation; no simulated video insight. Do not assume that lack of online stats means a player lacks quality. Test the pipeline against a simple goals/minutes baseline on sufficiently complete data before claiming discovery advantage.

## Next independent validation protocol (not done)
For each shortlisted player: three full matches, two independent observers with timestamps, at least 600 verified minutes or documented small-sample warning, team-style context, 24-month development tracking, explicit decision ledger and error analysis. No ML claim without legally usable longitudinal data.
